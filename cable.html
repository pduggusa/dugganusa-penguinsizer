How it is built. Device-to-manager allowance at both ends (400 mm each) + rise from each U to the pathway + |rack A − rack B| × pitch + a service loop each end, × the slack factor, rounded up to the next stocked length (0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 50 m). 1U = 44.45 mm.
What it is not. Lengths only — it has no opinion on media, reach or cost, and it does not know your containment, fill ratio or bend radius. Sizing-grade. Walk the route before you order in volume.
