Bill of materials

How the length is built. Device-to-manager allowance at both ends (400 mm each) + rise from each U to the pathway + |rack A − rack B| × pitch + a service loop each end, × the slack factor, rounded up to the next stocked length (0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 50 m). 1U = 44.45 mm. Unchanged from the lengths-only version, and identical to the sizer's ladder.
How the power is built. Per-phase A = kW × 1000 ÷ (V × √3 × PF); breaker headroom at 80% continuous. The single-zone-loss check assumes every dual-corded device fails over to its surviving feed at full load, which is what dual-corded means and is the only assumption that makes A/B worth paying for. Single-corded devices in a lost zone are counted as dropped, because they are.
What it is not. Not an electrical or mechanical stamp, not a quote, and it has no opinion on media reach — that lives in the sizer, deliberately in one place. Sizing-grade. Walk the route and read the as-built before you order in volume. Confidence capped at 95%.
