Reasoning is an inference load

Blackwell Ultra (B300) exists for reasoning. Reasoning models don't just train big — they burn compute at generation time: long chains of thought, test-time scaling, agentic loops. That moves the cost center from a one-off training run to a permanent inference load — cheapest on iron you own, not rented per-token. The 288 GB HBM and FP4 throughput are built to serve exactly that.

Sovereignty

Sovereign AI is the fastest-growing driver of on-prem GPU demand: nations and enterprises require models trained and served on infrastructure they own and control, in-country. A Penguin on-prem AI factory keeps weights, data, and inference inside your walls — no hyperscaler tenancy, no cross-border data flow, no waiting in someone else's capacity queue.

Compliance by architecture

Regulated data can't live in shared cloud. Healthcare (HIPAA), finance, and government (FedRAMP High / IL5+, EU AI Act, GDPR residency) need air-gap, data residency, and auditability a multi-tenant hyperscaler can't guarantee. On-prem brings the model to the data, not the data to the model — compliance as a property of the build, not a bolt-on.
